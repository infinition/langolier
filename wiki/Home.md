# Langolier Wiki

**[infinition.github.io/langolier](https://infinition.github.io/langolier/)**

Langolier is a local knowledge studio. You give it documents, code, videos and voice
notes; it indexes them and answers your questions with citations pointing at the exact
passage that backed each claim. Nothing leaves the machine unless you point it at a
cloud API.

New here? Start with [Installation](Installation), then [First steps](First-Steps).

## Language

The interface ships in English and French. The switch sits at the bottom of the
sidebar and the choice persists. This wiki is written in English; the README carries
a full French section.
